The JMCFI Accreditation Management System is used for accreditation, quality assurance, and related administrative purposes only. Information you submit — including uploaded evidence and quality-assurance responses — is stored in the system's institutional database, is protected with role-based access control, and is used solely to support accreditation reviews, internal quality monitoring, and related reporting.

The system's AI-assisted Smart Companion (AIRA) may summarize and assist on records that you are authorized to access. AIRA is advisory only, is not an accreditation decision-maker, and does not disclose your submitted data outside the system.

Please do not submit data that is not authorized, unrelated, or beyond the scope of the accreditation system.
