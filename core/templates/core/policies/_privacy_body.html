01Introduction

JMCFI is committed to protecting the privacy of students, faculty, staff, offices, and other individuals who use the Accreditation Management System. This Data Privacy Notice and Policy explains the personal data that the system processes, why it is processed, how it is protected, and the rights of the individuals who contribute to the accreditation process.

This notice applies to everyone who registers for, is assigned to, or uses the JMCFI AMS, including program heads, deans, area chairs, the Quality Assurance Office, administrators, and authorized external reviewers.

02Scope and Application

The system is operated for the legitimate internal purpose of preparing, managing, reviewing, and monitoring institutional accreditation evidence. Only approved users with an assigned internal role and department may be granted access, and all access is limited by user role, department scope, and assigned areas.

03Governing Law and Framework

Processing of personal data within the system is guided by the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules and regulations, together with applicable issuances of the National Privacy Commission. JMCFI applies these principles to accreditation evidence management:

  • Transparency — individuals are informed about how their data is processed.
  • Legitimate purpose — data is processed only for the accreditation purposes stated in this notice.
  • Proportionality — only the data necessary for the stated purposes is collected.

04What the Accreditation Management System Is

The JMCFI AMS organizes accreditation evidence in a hierarchical structure: Cycle → Level → Area → Sub-area → Requirement → Submission. Users prepare evidence requirements, attach supporting files, self-evaluate, and submit their work for review by deans, area chairs, and the Quality Assurance Office. Every important action is recorded in an audit trail so the institution can demonstrate accountability.

05Personal Data We Process

The system processes the following personal data when an account is created and used:

  • Account identity — name, institutional username or email address, and role requested.
  • Role and assignment data — internal role, department, area assignments, assigned deadlines, and approval status.
  • Profile data — optional profile photograph and communication details you choose to provide.
  • Activity records — evidence created or updated, submissions, review decisions, remarks, version history, notifications, and communications within the system.
  • Authentication events — login attempts and session activity, limited to what is necessary to protect the system.

06Sensitive Personal Data in Evidence

Accreditation evidence may legitimately include documents that contain personal or sensitive personal data of third parties (for example, student records, rosters, credentials, or performance information). JMCFI processes these documents strictly for accreditation purposes and limits access to the authorized reviewers and offices assigned to the relevant evidence.

Users are expected to upload only documents that are necessary and authorized for the accreditation workspace, and to avoid including unnecessary personal or sensitive data wherever possible.

07Sources of Personal Data

  • You, directly — through account registration, profile management, evidence submissions, remarks, and messages.
  • Authorized administrators — when approving accounts and assigning roles, departments, and areas.
  • Institutional accreditation activity — evidence documents and review records prepared as part of the accreditation workflow.

08Purposes of Processing

Personal data is processed for the following purposes:

  • Managing and monitoring accreditation evidence for institutional quality assurance.
  • Assigning the correct responsibilities to each role and department.
  • Facilitating internal review, revision, and approval workflows.
  • Providing accountability through audit records and version history.
  • Producing readiness, compliance, and monitoring reports used by the institution.
  • Supporting communication between authorized accreditation offices.
  • Protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the system.

11Who May Access Personal Data

Access to personal data is role-based and strictly limited to what each role needs to perform its accreditation responsibilities:

  • Program Heads — evidence for their assigned program and department.
  • Deans and Area Chairs — evidence within their department scope and assigned areas.
  • Quality Assurance Office and Accreditation Head — evidence and reporting across the accreditation cycle for monitoring purposes.
  • Administrators — account administration, security, and configuration.
  • Authorized external reviewers — only the evidence approved for external review within the defined access scope.

All access decisions are enforced by the system. Audit logs record who acted on which evidence and when.

12Disclosure and Sharing

The system does not sell, rent, or trade personal data. Personal data collected within the AMS is shared only:

  • internally with the authorized offices and roles described in this notice; or
  • with accredited external bodies (for example, PACUCOA) strictly within the scope authorized by JMCFI for accreditation review.

13Third-Party Services

Optional Google sign-in uses Google's OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect flow. When you choose to sign in with Google, Google verifies the email address on our behalf. JMCFI does not receive your Google password. The system does not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

14Security Measures

JMCFI applies organizational and technical safeguards to protect personal data, including:

  • Role-based access control that limits data to authorized users.
  • Encrypted database, backup, and transport protections where applicable.
  • Hashed and salted authentication credentials.
  • Complete audit logging of important account, evidence, and review actions.
  • Rate limiting and security headers to resist automated attacks.
  • Session controls consistent with best practice for institutional web applications.

No security measure is infallible. If a data incident is identified, JMCFI will respond in accordance with applicable privacy and incident-response procedures.

15Your Rights Under the Data Privacy Act

Under Republic Act No. 10173, users have the right to:

  • Access their personal data held by the system.
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Remove or block data that should no longer be processed.
  • Object to processing in the lawful cases provided.
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.
  • Be indemnified for damages sustained due to unlawful or unauthorized processing, as allowed by law.

16How to Exercise Your Rights

Requests to access, correct, or remove data may be addressed to the institution's Data Protection Officer. Requests should identify the data subject, describe the request, and state the relevant data or system records concerned. JMCFI will act on valid requests in accordance with applicable privacy rules.

17Automated Processing and Advisory Tools

The AIRA Smart Companion provides AI-assisted information and recommendations based on authorized system data. It is an advisory tool only. It does not make, approve, or finalize any accreditation decision. Decisions remain with the authorized offices and reviewers.

18Cookies, Sessions, and Local Storage

The system uses a session cookie to keep you signed in during your visit. Your last selected visual theme is stored locally in your browser so the interface can load without a flash of the wrong theme. No tracking is performed. Evidence, submissions, approvals, and review decisions are never stored in browser local storage.

19User Responsibilities

Every user of the system is expected to:

  • Upload only documents authorized for the accreditation workspace.
  • Limit uploaded content to what the requirement actually needs.
  • Keep account credentials confidential and report suspected compromise.
  • Use system data only for official accreditation purposes.
  • Report suspected data incidents to the contacts in this notice.

20Incident Reporting

Suspected privacy incidents involving the Accreditation Management System — including unauthorized access, loss of device or credentials, or systems that behave unexpectedly — should be reported promptly to the contact information in Section 23 so JMCFI can take appropriate protective action.

21Policy Review and Updates

This notice may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the system, accreditation activities, or applicable privacy rules. The version shown at the top of this page indicates the current policy. Material changes will be communicated through the portal and will take effect on the date published with the updated notice.

22Acknowledgment

By requesting access to, or continuing to use, the JMCFI Accreditation Management System, you acknowledge that you have read this Data Privacy Notice and Policy and understand how personal data is processed within the accreditation workflow. You also affirm that you will handle the personal data of other individuals with confidentiality and care.

Your explicit acknowledgment of each policy version is recorded in the system and is used to confirm that you accepted the policy version in effect at the time. You may review your acknowledgment history at any time under Settings → Privacy & Legal.
