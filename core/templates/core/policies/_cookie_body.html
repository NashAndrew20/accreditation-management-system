01Overview

This Cookie Policy explains how the Jose Maria College Foundation, Inc. (JMCFI) Accreditation Management System (AMS) uses cookies and similar storage technologies. It describes the essential cookies required for the system to work securely, and the optional cookies the system currently uses — if any.

The system does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies. Your acknowledgment of this policy is recorded server-side together with the policy version and the date it was acknowledged.

02Essential Cookies

Essential cookies are required for secure sign-in, session management, and core system functionality. They are always active and cannot be disabled from the AMS, because doing so would prevent the system from operating securely. They are limited to the minimum necessary for the system to function:

  • Session cookie — keeps you signed in during your visit and prevents unauthorized impersonation.
  • CSRF / security cookie — protects state-changing requests (forms, submissions, approvals) from cross-site request forgery.

03Optional Cookies

The AMS currently uses no optional cookies. No analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies are deployed, and the system does not use third-party trackers.

Your last selected visual theme is stored in your browser's local storage (not in a cookie) so the interface can load without a flash of the wrong theme. Evidence, submissions, approvals, and review decisions are never stored in cookies or browser local storage.

04Managing Cookies in Your Browser

Because essential cookies are required for the AMS to function securely, you can block or delete cookies through your browser settings, but doing so will prevent you from signing in and using the system. Instructions are provided by your browser's documentation.

05Policy Updates and Acknowledgment

This Cookie Policy may be updated when the system or its cookie usage changes. The version shown indicates the current policy. When an updated version is published, users are asked to acknowledge the new version before continuing to use the system.
