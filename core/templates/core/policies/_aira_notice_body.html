01What AIRA Is

AIRA is the AI-assisted Smart Companion inside the JMCFI Accreditation Management System (AMS). AIRA answers natural-language questions about accreditation work and produces summaries, assistance, insights, and recommendations to help authorized users organize evidence and stay on top of review activity.

02Data AIRA May Use

AIRA processes only the accreditation records and uploaded evidence that are available to the authenticated user through the AMS, within the scope of that user's role, department, and assigned areas. This includes evidence submissions, review statuses, deadlines, and the active accreditation structure relevant to the user's access.

AIRA enforces the same role-based access control (RBAC) and permissions as the rest of the system. It never accesses, summarizes, or confirms records outside the user's authorization scope.

03Advisory Only

AIRA is an advisory tool. It does not make, approve, or finalize any accreditation decision, and it is not the final accreditation decision-maker. Final decisions remain with the authorized offices, reviewers, and administrators.

Verify before relying. AI-generated information should be verified by authorized personnel against the official system records before any decision is made or communicated.

04Processing Location and Data Sharing

AIRA derives every reply from the authorized AMS records described above using the system's own in-application intelligence engine. No external AI-provider call is made, and AIRA does not transmit your questions or any system records to an external provider. Unauthorized records are never exposed, and uploaded document contents are treated as data, never as instructions.

05User Responsibilities

  • Use AIRA only for legitimate accreditation, quality assurance, and related administrative purposes.
  • Never ask AIRA to reveal records outside your authorized scope.
  • Verify AI-generated summaries and recommendations against official records before acting on them.
  • Treat AI-generated assistance as guidance, not as an approval or official decision.
